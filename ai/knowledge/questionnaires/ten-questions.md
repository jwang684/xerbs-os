# TCM Ten Questions (十问)

## Purpose

The Ten Questions form the foundation of every clinical assessment.

The AI Engine uses these questions to collect structured patient information before syndrome differentiation.

---

# 1. Chief Complaint (问主诉)

## Collect

- Primary concern
- Duration
- Severity
- Previous diagnosis
- Previous treatment

---

# 2. Cold & Heat (问寒热)

## Collect

- Feeling cold
- Feeling hot
- Fever
- Chills
- Sweating preference
- Cold hands or feet
- Night sweats
- Hot flashes

---

# 3. Head & Body (问身)

## Collect

- Headache
- Dizziness
- Fatigue
- Body pain
- Joint pain
- Muscle soreness
- Numbness
- Weakness

---

# 4. Appetite & Digestion (问饮食)

## Collect

- Appetite
- Thirst
- Preferred drinks
- Digestion
- Bloating
- Nausea
- Acid reflux
- Stool frequency
- Stool consistency

---

# 5. Urination (问二便)

## Collect

- Urination frequency
- Urination color
- Painful urination
- Night urination
- Urgency
- Incontinence

---

# 6. Sleep (问睡眠)

## Collect

- Sleep quality
- Sleep duration
- Difficulty falling asleep
- Night waking
- Dream disturbance
- Early waking

---

# 7. Emotions (问情志)

## Collect

- Stress
- Anxiety
- Depression
- Irritability
- Mood swings
- Emotional triggers

---

# 8. Women's Health (问妇科)

## Collect

- Menstrual cycle
- Cycle length
- Flow
- Blood color
- Clots
- Pain
- Pregnancy history
- Menopause

---

# 9. Respiratory & Other Symptoms

## Collect

- Cough
- Phlegm
- Chest tightness
- Shortness of breath
- Palpitations
- Skin problems

---

# 10. Medical History (问既往史)

## Collect

- Chronic diseases
- Surgeries
- Medications
- Supplements
- Allergies
- Family history

---

# Output

The questionnaire should produce structured findings only.

Example:

- fatigue
- poor appetite
- bloating
- loose stool
- insomnia
- anxiety
- cold extremities

These findings are passed to the Diagnosis Engine.
