# Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the Xerbs product ecosystem.

It defines the vision, scope, users, core capabilities, and guiding principles of the Xerbs platform. It serves as the entry point for all product documentation and establishes a shared understanding across product, engineering, design, operations, and clinical teams.

---

# Product Vision

Xerbs delivers personalized herbal healthcare through an integrated digital platform that connects patients, providers, operations, and intelligent decision support into one continuous healthcare experience.

Rather than building isolated software, Xerbs creates a connected ecosystem that supports long-term health management.

---

# Product Mission

Build digital products that enable personalized healthcare to be:

- Accessible
- Intelligent
- Continuous
- Efficient
- Trusted

---

# Product Principles

Every product developed by Xerbs should follow these principles.

## Personalized by Default

Every recommendation, workflow, and experience should adapt to the individual.

---

## Continuous Care

Healthcare is an ongoing relationship rather than isolated appointments.

---

## Human-Centered

Technology supports healthcare professionals rather than replacing them.

---

## Simplicity

Complex healthcare processes should feel simple for users.

---

## Security & Privacy

Customer data must always be protected through secure and compliant systems.

# Product Ecosystem

The Xerbs platform consists of four connected experiences.

## Patient Experience

Products designed for patients.

Examples include:

- Patient Portal
- Health Assessment
- Appointment Scheduling
- Treatment Plans
- Health Reports
- Progress Tracking

---

## Provider Experience

Products designed for healthcare providers.

Examples include:

- Electronic Health Records
- Clinical Notes
- Formula Builder
- Prescription Management
- Patient Timeline
- Decision Support

---

## Operations Experience

Products supporting business operations.

Examples include:

- Scheduling
- Billing
- Inventory
- Order Management
- CRM
- Analytics

---

## Intelligence Platform

Shared intelligent capabilities used across the entire ecosystem.

Examples include:

- Xerbs OS
- AI Assistant
- Formula Intelligence
- Health Memory
- Workflow Automation
- Knowledge Engine

# Xerbs OS

Xerbs OS is the internal intelligence platform that powers the Xerbs ecosystem.

Customers interact with Xerbs.

Healthcare providers interact with specialized clinical tools.

Operations teams use business management systems.

Behind every experience, Xerbs OS coordinates data, workflows, intelligence, and automation.
