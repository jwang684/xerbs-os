# Xerbs Product Specification

## 1. Product Positioning

Xerbs is an AI-powered operating system for personalized herbal healthcare.

Unlike traditional symptom checkers or one-time AI consultations, Xerbs focuses on the entire treatment journey—from assessment and herbal formula generation to continuous care and long-term outcome tracking.

---

## 2. Product Philosophy

Xerbs is designed around one principle:

> Continuous herbal care, not one-time diagnosis.

The platform helps users:

- Understand their health
- Receive personalized herbal recommendations
- Follow treatment plans
- Track progress
- Continuously optimize care

## 4. Core Modules

### Dashboard

The personalized home page showing today's treatment plan, reminders, progress, and AI insights.

### Diagnose

Collect symptoms, tongue images, questionnaires, and generate clinical assessments.

### Formula

Generate personalized herbal formulas, explain recommendations, and manage formula versions.

### Care

Support daily treatment tracking, reminders, and recovery monitoring.

### Herbs

Provide a structured herbal knowledge base with explanations, safety information, and evidence.

### Profile

Maintain long-term health records and Health Memory.
