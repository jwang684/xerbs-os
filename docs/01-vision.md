# Xerbs Vision

## Vision

Build the world's leading AI-powered platform for personalized herbal healthcare, empowering individuals with intelligent diagnosis, evidence-informed treatment planning, and continuous care.

## Mission

Empower individuals with AI-driven herbal diagnosis, personalized treatment planning, and continuous health management.

## Why Xerbs

Healthcare today is fragmented.

People receive isolated diagnoses, disconnected treatment plans, and little long-term guidance.

Xerbs aims to connect diagnosis, herbal medicine, treatment, and continuous care into one intelligent platform.

## Core Principles

1. Clinical safety before AI capability.
2. Personalized herbal care, not generic healthcare.
3. Continuous care over episodic diagnosis.
4. Explainable AI recommendations.
5. Continuous learning through Health Memory.

## Target Users

- Individuals seeking personalized herbal care.
- Patients undergoing long-term herbal treatment.
- TCM practitioners and clinics.

- ## Design Principles

- Clinical safety first
- Explainable AI
- Evidence-informed recommendations
- Personalized treatment
- Long-term health management

## Success Metrics

- High diagnostic consistency
- High patient engagement
- Improved treatment adherence
- Measurable patient outcomes
- Long-term user retention
- High user trust in AI recommendations
