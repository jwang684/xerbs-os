# Purpose

This document defines the ideal customer journey within the Xerbs ecosystem.

It describes how customers discover, experience, and continue their personalized healthcare journey through Xerbs. The goal is to ensure every interaction is consistent, personalized, and focused on long-term health outcomes.

---

# Journey Philosophy

Healthcare should not end after a consultation or a purchase.

Every interaction should build upon previous experiences, creating a continuous relationship between the customer and Xerbs.

The customer journey is designed around long-term care rather than one-time transactions.

---

# Customer Journey Overview

The ideal Xerbs journey consists of seven stages:

1. Discover
2. Learn
3. Assess
4. Receive Care
5. Improve
6. Maintain
7. Grow

Each stage strengthens trust and supports continuous health improvement.

---

# Stage 1 — Discover

Customers first become aware of Xerbs through various channels.

Examples include:

- Website
- Search engines
- Social media
- Educational content
- Professional referrals
- Family and friends

### Customer Goal

Find a trustworthy provider of personalized herbal healthcare.

### Xerbs Goal

Build credibility and encourage exploration.

---

# Stage 2 — Learn

Customers learn about Xerbs, its philosophy, and its approach to personalized healthcare.

They explore:

- Services
- Products
- Healthcare philosophy
- Practitioner information
- Frequently asked questions

### Customer Goal

Understand how Xerbs is different.

### Xerbs Goal

Build trust through education and transparency.

---

# Stage 3 — Assess

The customer begins a personalized health assessment.

This may include:

- Health questionnaire
- Medical history
- Lifestyle information
- Symptoms
- Health goals
- Consultation

### Customer Goal

Receive an accurate understanding of their health.

### Xerbs Goal

Develop a comprehensive understanding of the individual.

---

# Stage 4 — Receive Care

Based on the assessment, Xerbs provides personalized recommendations.

Examples include:

- Personalized herbal formula
- Consultation
- Health guidance
- Lifestyle recommendations
- Educational resources

### Customer Goal

Begin treatment with confidence.

### Xerbs Goal

Deliver personalized and evidence-informed care.

---

# Stage 5 — Improve

Customers monitor progress over time.

Activities may include:

- Progress tracking
- Follow-up consultations
- Formula adjustments
- Symptom updates
- Health reports

### Customer Goal

See measurable improvements.

### Xerbs Goal

Continuously optimize care.

---

# Stage 6 — Maintain

Once health goals have improved, the relationship continues.

Services may include:

- Preventive care
- Wellness programs
- Periodic assessments
- Long-term monitoring
- Personalized recommendations

### Customer Goal

Maintain long-term health.

### Xerbs Goal

Support lifelong wellness.

---

# Stage 7 — Grow

Satisfied customers become long-term members of the Xerbs community.

They may:

- Continue receiving care
- Recommend Xerbs to others
- Participate in wellness programs
- Explore additional services

### Customer Goal

Build lasting confidence in their health.

### Xerbs Goal

Create lifelong relationships built on trust.

---

# Digital Experience Throughout the Journey

Digital tools support every stage of the customer journey.

Examples include:

- Patient Portal
- Appointment Scheduling
- Health Reports
- Progress Tracking
- Secure Messaging
- Notifications
- Personalized Recommendations

Technology should simplify the experience rather than replace human care.

Personalized
