# Purpose

This document defines how Xerbs creates, delivers, and captures value.

It explains who our customers are, what problems we solve, how we differentiate ourselves, and how our business is designed for sustainable long-term growth.

This document complements the Company Blueprint by focusing specifically on the company's commercial strategy.

---

# 1. Business Overview

Xerbs is a personalized herbal healthcare company that combines herbal expertise, intelligent technology, and continuous health management to deliver individualized healthcare experiences.

Rather than selling standardized products, Xerbs provides personalized health solutions that evolve with each customer over time.

---

# 2. Problem We Solve

Modern healthcare faces several challenges:

- One-size-fits-all treatment
- Fragmented health information
- Limited personalization
- Reactive rather than preventive care
- Lack of continuous follow-up
- Difficulty translating complex health data into actionable care

Customers often receive isolated treatments instead of long-term healthcare guidance.

---

# 3. Our Solution

Xerbs addresses these challenges through an integrated healthcare ecosystem that combines:

- Personalized herbal products
- Professional consultation
- Continuous health management
- Personalized health guidance
- Long-term Health Memory

Each interaction contributes to a continuously improving understanding of the customer.

---

# 4. Value Proposition

For every customer, Xerbs delivers:

- Personalized care
- Trusted herbal expertise
- Continuous health management
- Professional support
- Long-term health optimization

Customers do not simply purchase products.

They build an ongoing healthcare relationship.
