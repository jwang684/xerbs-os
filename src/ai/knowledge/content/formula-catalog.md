# Formula Catalog — Canonical Names

**Knowledge Content Sprint · Step 6 (Formula Catalog Authoring — Batch 1).**
Status: **Approved and frozen (2026-08-06).** This is authored knowledge content for the
`formulaCatalog` Vocabulary interface: the canonical name-space of formulas,
identity only. It is a representation-neutral list of canonical names; it is not
yet wired to the loader/registry/contract (that is a later step) and contains no
meaning, aliases, translations, dosage, composition, metadata, or relationships.

**Canonical naming convention (editorial standard applied to every entry):**
pinyin romanization, Title Case, syllable-separated, no tone marks, English
translation excluded (translation is the deferred localization layer). Each entry
is exactly one canonical name and nothing else. One entry per identity.
