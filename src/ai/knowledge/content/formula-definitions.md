# Formula Definitions — Treatment-Principle Meanings

**Formula Definitions · Content Sprint · Authoring (v1 — Treatment Principles).**
Status: **PROPOSED — PENDING REVIEW.** This is authored knowledge content for the
`formulaDefinitions` interface: the strategy-tier **meaning** layer. Each entry pairs a
**referenced canonical treatment-principle identity** (owned by
`formulaDefinitionCatalog`, shown in bold) with an **objective explanation** of that
principle's intrinsic therapeutic meaning. It is not yet wired to the
loader/registry/contract (a later step).

**Entry model (per the frozen Specification `docs/architecture/39`):** exactly two
conceptual fields — a referenced canonical treatment principle and its objective
explanation. Nothing else.

**Definition Neutrality / Intrinsic Therapeutic Identity (as applied here):** a
definition states only *what the treatment strategy fundamentally does* — its therapeutic
direction, intent, and effect. It never states when or whether to use the principle,
which diagnostic patterns call for it, which formulas realize it, which diseases benefit,
or any symptom, recommendation, ranking, reasoning, dosage, or mapping. It names no
patterns, syndromes, diseases, formulas, or herbs.

Every referenced principle exists in the frozen `formula-definition-catalog.md` (v1 —
treatment principles only).

## Definitions

- **Release Wind-Cold** — A treatment principle that disperses wind and cold from the exterior through warming, outward-dispersing action.
- **Release Wind-Heat** — A treatment principle that disperses wind and heat from the exterior through cooling, outward-venting action.
