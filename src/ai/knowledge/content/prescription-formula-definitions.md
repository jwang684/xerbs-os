# Prescription Formula Definitions

**Knowledge Content Sprint · Prescription Formula Definitions · Step 4 (Definition
Authoring — Batch 1).** Status: **Approved and frozen (2026-08-06).** This is authored
knowledge content for the `prescriptionFormulaDefinitions` Definition interface:
the meaning of a prescription formula. It is a representation-neutral list; it is
not yet wired to the loader/registry/contract (a later step) and contains no
identity, mapping, reasoning, execution, dosage, composition, recommendation,
citation, or metadata.

**Entry model (per the frozen Specification):** each entry is exactly two
conceptual fields — a **referenced canonical formula** (a name owned by
`formulaCatalog`, shown in bold) and an **objective explanation** (its meaning).
Nothing else.

**Definition Neutrality (as applied here):** a definition states only *what a
formula is* — its objective, timeless therapeutic nature/action. It never states
when or why to use it, what patient condition it treats, whether it is preferred,
better, first-line, commonly used, or how it should be selected, and never its
composition, dosage, or administration. *Interpretation note: "what it treats"
(forbidden) is read as patient-facing indication/use; a formula's intrinsic action
is read as part of its meaning.*

Every referenced formula already exists in the frozen Formula Catalog (Batch 1).
