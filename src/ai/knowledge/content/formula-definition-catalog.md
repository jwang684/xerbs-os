# Formula Definition Catalog — Canonical Treatment Principles

**Formula Definition Catalog · Content Sprint · Authoring (v1 — Treatment Principles).**
Status: **PROPOSED — PENDING REVIEW.** This is authored knowledge content for the
`formulaDefinitionCatalog` Vocabulary interface: the identity root of the strategy tier,
identity only. It is a representation-neutral list of canonical treatment-principle
names; it is not yet wired to the loader/registry/contract (a later step) and contains no
meaning, definitions, mappings, formula families, named formulas, herbs, diagnosis
patterns, categories, aliases, translations, or metadata.

**v1 scope (per `docs/architecture/37`):** treatment principles only. Formula families are
deferred and excluded from v1; they may be added later as an additive expansion without
disturbing these identities.

**Canonical naming convention (editorial standard applied to every entry):** the canonical
therapeutic-method phrase in English Title Case, one canonical representation per identity
(short connecting words such as "and", "the", "from" in lower case). Each entry is exactly
one canonical name and nothing else. One entry per identity; aliases and alternate
phrasings are not separate entries.
